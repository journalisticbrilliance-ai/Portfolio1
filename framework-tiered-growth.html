Growth Framework

Customer Success Operating Framework

Every engagement model is designed to ensure customers receive the right level of partnership based on their business impact, lifecycle stage, and growth potential, while giving the Customer Success team a scalable operating model.

Accounts are tiered primarily by revenue. Within each tier, risk, lifecycle stage, complexity, and growth signals determine the level of intervention, with clear handoffs across Sales, Implementation, Customer Success, and Support. Staffing, sustainability, and incentives are defined for every tier, not only the long tail.

Thresholds, SLAs, tier values, and trigger windows throughout are illustrative and should be validated against customer behavior, contract structure, team capacity, and historical retention data. This is an operating model, not a fixed answer.

🗂 Every account gets a tier, a set of KPIs, and an owner. Nobody has to renegotiate the relationship call by call
